JWC FIELD APP — PHONE SETUP
Save this guide and forward it to new employees.

APP LINK
https://www.jwchauling.com/app/

BEFORE YOU START
You need cell service or Wi-Fi. If the app asks for a Google account, contact Jordan so the employee access setting can be corrected.

IPHONE (SAFARI)
1. Open Safari — the blue compass app.
2. Type or paste the APP LINK above into the address bar and open it.
3. Wait until the JWC welcome screen and truck photo appear.
4. Tap Share — the square with an arrow pointing up. Depending on your Safari version, it may be inside the menu beside the address bar.
5. Scroll through the options and tap Add to Home Screen.
6. Name it JWC Field App, then tap Add.
7. The JWC Field App shortcut is now on your home screen. Tap it to open the form.

ANDROID (CHROME)
1. Open Chrome.
2. Type or paste the APP LINK above into the address bar and open it.
3. Wait until the JWC welcome screen and truck photo appear.
4. Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome.
5. Tap Add to Home screen. If Chrome shows Install app instead, tap that.
6. Name it JWC Field App if asked, then tap Add or Install. Confirm if prompted.
7. The JWC Field App shortcut is now on your home screen. Tap it to open the form.

EACH TIME YOU USE IT
1. Make sure your phone has cell service or Wi-Fi.
2. Open JWC Field App from your home screen. Tap Field Tickets or Maintenance.
3. Complete the required fields, then tap Submit.
4. Wait for confirmation that it was sent to Google Sheets before closing it. If an error appears, keep the form open and contact Jordan.
