Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2019

JWC Contracting Ltd. ("JWC", "we") runs this website, its employee field app, and an internal billing tool that connects to JWC's own QuickBooks Online account. This policy explains what information those tools handle.

Website visitors

The public website does not use accounts, advertising trackers or analytics cookies. If you email or call us, we use your contact details only to respond to you.

Employee field app

JWC employees use the field app to submit work tickets and truck maintenance records (dates, customer, job, truck, hours, work description, driver name, odometer). These records are stored in JWC's private Google Sheets workbook and used for dispatch, maintenance and billing.

QuickBooks connection

JWC's internal billing tool connects only to JWC's own QuickBooks Online company. It reads JWC's customer, product/service and tax lists, and creates or updates JWC's own unsent invoices from field tickets. It does not send invoices, process payments, or access any other company's data. QuickBooks sign-in tokens are stored privately in JWC's Google account and can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers that host it (Google, Intuit, GitHub) or when required by law.
